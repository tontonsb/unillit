Reading tips for place names
Stress & vowel reduction
Unstressed о is often pronounced like a: Москва sounds like Mas-KVA. Romanisations write o regardless.
Soft sign ь
Palatalises the consonant before it — shown as apostrophe in strict romanisation (Пермь → Perm'), often dropped in everyday use.
Ё ё vs Е е
The two dots (diaeresis) are optional in Russian text — both may appear as е. BGN/PCGN writes Е as ye after a vowel or at word start, e elsewhere; Ё always as yo.
Serbian: two scripts
Serbian uses Cyrillic and Latin interchangeably. The Latin equivalent is always derivable: Београд = Beograd.
Romanisation follows BGN/PCGN 1947 for Russian. Other languages use their own national romanisation standards; mappings shown are simplified for toponym reading. Cyrillic Unicode blocks: U+0400–U+04FF (Slavic) · U+0500–U+052F (supplement)
