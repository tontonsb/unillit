Alif & hamza variants
Alif ا is non-connecting. Hamza ء (glottal stop) sits on a seat or alone.
ا alif ā / a
أ alif + hamza above ʾa / ʾu
إ alif + hamza below ʾi
آ alif maddah ʾā
ء hamza (alone) ʾ
ئ hamza on ya seat ʾ
ؤ hamza on waw seat ʾ
ة tāʾ marbūṭa h / t
tāʾ marbūṭa ة — looks like ه with 2 dots; only word-final; romanised h in pause, t in construct (Madīnat al-Nūr). In many place names simply written as a in English.

The definite article الـ and sun / moon letters
☀ Sun letters (14) — الـ assimilates: al- → a[x]-
ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن
◑ Moon letters (14) — الـ stays al-
ب ج ح خ ع غ ف ق ك م ه و ي + ا
Examples  ·  the article ال is always written in full, only the pronunciation assimilates:
الشَّمْس → ash-shams (the sun) · ش is a sun letter
النِّيل → an-Nīl (the Nile) · ن is a sun letter
القَاهِرَة → al-Qāhira (Cairo) · ق is a moon letter
الجَبَل → al-Jabal (the mountain) · ج is a moon letter
