Reading tips for place names
Inherent vowel
Every consonant has an inherent o (Bengali realises Sanskrit a as o). At word-end it is often silent: ঢাকা → Dhaka, not Dhakao.
Aspirated stops
kh gh ch jh ṭh ḍh th dh ph bh are single consonants with a puff of air — not two-letter clusters as in English.
Conjuncts
Two consonants without a vowel between them form a conjunct ligature, often unrecognisable from its parts. Common in place names: ক্ষ (kṣ/kh), জ্ঞ (gñ/gg).
Romanisation follows a practical IAST-lite scheme suited for toponyms (ṭ ḍ ṇ ṣ ṛ diacritics mark retroflex/special sounds; ī ū mark long vowels). Scholarly ISO 15919 differs slightly. বাংলা script · Unicode block U+0980–U+09FF
